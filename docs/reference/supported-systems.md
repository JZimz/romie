# Supported Systems

These are the systems ROMie can identify and organize. Whether a system can be synced to your device depends on your firmware profile, check [Supported Devices](/devices/supported-devices) for per-device details.

| SNES                      | `.sfc`, `.snes`, `.zip`, `.7z`                                |

## Notes

Compressed formats (`.zip`, `.7z`) are supported for most systems. Whether the handheld firmware can read compressed ROMs depends on the emulator, if a game doesn't load on your device, try the uncompressed format.

Nintendo DS hashing for RetroAchievements identification is currently limited. DS games will still import, but may not match against the RA database.
