# Your Library

## Browsing

The sidebar gives you a few ways to get at your games:

**By system**: ROMie groups your games by console. Tap any system to see all the games you have for it. You can reorder systems in Settings if you want your most-used ones at the top.

**Recently Added**: the last games you imported, newest first. Handy for finding what you just added without hunting through your whole library.

**Favorites**: games you've starred. Open any game and hit the favorite button to add it here.

**Tags**: your custom playlists. See [Tags](/library/tags) for how those work.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar at the top to find games by name. Results update as you type.

## Library stats

ROMie tracks how many games you have across all systems. You can see a breakdown in the stats view, total games, games per system, and how much storage they're taking up.
